## Evidence discipline

- “Confirmed” means the capability is explicitly described on a public source.
- “Claimed” means the competitor markets the capability but the underlying implementation or quality was not independently tested.
- “Unknown” means the public surface does not establish the behavior; Znip should use an explicit product decision or hands-on benchmark.
- Do not copy competitor code, assets, branding, text, UI, or proprietary interaction details. Build behaviorally compatible primitives with an original product language.
