# CleanShot X — Detailed Research

## Executive read

CleanShot is the mature benchmark: excellent Mac-native capture ergonomics, deep annotation, scrolling capture, recording overlays, video editing, OCR, floating references, history, and an established Cloud/team product.

Observed pricing: **CleanShot Basic $35 one-time**, including one year of updates and 1 GB Cloud; optional renewal shown as $19/year. **CleanShot Pro $10/user/month billed annually** or a monthly plan, with unlimited Cloud storage, custom domain/branding, advanced sharing/security, team management, SSO and SCIM. Both plans include the full Mac app. CleanShot requires macOS 13 or newer.

## Settings/customization

CleanShot states that users can customize shortcuts, capture behavior, file formats and sharing options. Znip should treat shortcut conflict handling, output format, save/copy/upload defaults, naming, history retention and permission prompts as a complete settings system rather than scattered toggles.
